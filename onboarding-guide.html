Welcome

You're in good company

North Columbus Cleaning Company is a local, professional cleaning service for homes and businesses across the North Columbus, Ohio area. We believe a clean, organized space makes everyday life better — and we get it right the first time. We're glad to have you on board.

Your role

About your role

As an independent contractor cleaner, you choose your assignments and set your own schedule. Your job is to deliver thorough, detail-oriented cleaning to homes and businesses — working independently, keeping client information confidential, and communicating professionally.

If you value flexibility and take pride in your work, you'll fit right in.

Supplies & equipment

Your toolkit

Booking Koala

Booking Koala keeps everything organized — your schedule, job details, and assignments, all in one place. Download the Booking Koala for Providers app from the Apple App Store or Google Play to manage your work: view your schedule, accept cleaning jobs, and track your progress.

Policies

Our commitment to job completion

We're committed to completing every job to a high standard. We pay by the job, not by the hour, to keep the focus on thoroughness and quality. If a job is left incomplete or doesn't meet the checklist, the cleaner returns to finish it. That's how we keep our clients happy and our reputation solid.
